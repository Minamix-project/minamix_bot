MinamixBot Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

MinamixBot is operated by Ichiky ("we", "us"). For privacy questions, contact minamix@ichdev.mozmail.com.

Data we process

To provide its features, MinamixBot may process Discord user, guild, channel and role IDs; usernames and display names; message content needed for message rewards and roleplay prefixes; economy balances and transactions; moderation warnings; AFK information; roleplay character names, prefixes and images; administrative audit records; and technical logs.

For reminders, we also store the creator's ID, destination channel, selected recipient IDs, reminder text, category, scheduled time, and delivery status. We use these details to deliver and manage reminders.

When administrators configure a general log channel, the bot sends records of message edits and deletions there. These records can include the message author's ID and name, channel and message IDs, timestamps, available message content before and after an edit, and attachment names and URLs. Long records and bulk deletions can include text transcripts.

For support tickets, we store panel settings, creator and linked-subject IDs, category, channel and support-role IDs, staff participation, creation/closure dates, closing actor and reason, previous access permissions, and an action history. Manual exports and closing snapshots store available message text, author/message IDs, timestamps, embed text and attachment links in MySQL. Attachment binaries, deleted messages, previous edits and thread histories are not included. Closing snapshots may also be sent to a configured private Discord log channel. Ticket records and exports remain until administrators use the confirmed /ticket purge command, remove them through database maintenance or act on a deletion request. Ticket purge also removes ticket channels and their retained message history, resets the server ticket counter, preserves the panel, and keeps a minimal purge audit record. Copies already sent to Discord logs or downloaded by recipients are not erased by this command; database backups follow their normal retention. The creator and authorized support staff can export a ticket only while they have source-channel access. Linking another user does not grant them access.

How we use data

We use this data to operate the bot, apply configured moderation and economy rules, display roleplay characters, prevent abuse, troubleshoot failures, maintain backups, and keep an audit trail of administrative actions. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

Storage and retention

Configured devlog channels, published public GitHub release metadata, and announcement message IDs are stored to update existing devlog posts across restarts. Devlogs are published only when an authorized administrator invokes the command.

Application data is stored in a MySQL database hosted by our infrastructure provider. Database backups are retained for up to 14 days. We keep data only while it is needed for the bot’s operation, security, legal obligations, or backup recovery.

Pending reminders remain stored until delivered or deleted. Sent and failed reminders are removed from the application database after 30 days while the bot is running. You can delete your pending or failed reminders with /reminder delete. Delivered Discord messages and database backups follow their own retention rules.

When general logs are configured, message history is also stored in MySQL to support moderation across restarts, unless administrators disable collection. Message bodies, usernames, attachment names and URLs, and message-origin classification are authenticated-encrypted with a key stored separately from the database. Guild, channel, message and author IDs, event types and timestamps remain queryable metadata. New capture, imports, and edit/deletion logs exclude direct messages and messages from bots, applications, and webhooks, including roleplay proxies. Events without an identifiable human origin are skipped. Previously retained records and Discord logs follow their existing retention rules.

Message history is retained for at most 30 days by default, with additional limits of 5,000 versions per server and 10 versions per message. Administrators can select 1–30 days and a server capacity of 100–20,000 versions. Expired versions are hidden immediately; physical cleanup runs every 15 minutes while the bot is online, including after startup. Oldest versions are also removed when a storage limit is reached. Explicit imports only retrieve current, still-existing messages within the retention window and do not reconstruct deleted content or previous edits.

Members with Administrator or Manage Server permission can consult retained versions privately only when they also have source-channel access; private threads require the appropriate access as well. Manage Messages alone does not grant access to these tools. Authorized administrators can purge selected history or disable and erase collection. Encrypted copies in SQL backups remain until the backup retention period ends. The resulting Discord log messages, text exports and transcript attachments follow Discord's storage and remain until removed by administrators or recipients. Original attachment files are not downloaded or reuploaded by this feature.

Discord and service providers

The bot uses Discord’s API and services. Images uploaded for roleplay characters may be stored as Discord message attachments. Hosting and database providers may process data on our behalf and are limited to providing the service.

Your choices and deletion requests

You may request access to or deletion of data associated with your Discord account by contacting minamix@ichdev.mozmail.com. We will verify the request and delete data that we are not required to retain. Server administrators may also request deletion of server configuration and bot data.

Security

We use access controls, restricted credentials, backups, and container security measures. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Children

MinamixBot is not intended to knowingly collect data from anyone under the minimum age required by applicable law.

Changes

We may update this policy when the bot or applicable requirements change. The latest version will always be published at this URL.
